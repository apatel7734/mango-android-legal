Delete your Mango account and data

App: Mango (com.farmlog.mango)
Developer: Ashish Patel

Mango stores all farm records locally on your Android device. There is no cloud account and no server-side copy of your data.

How to delete your account and all data

Choose one of these methods:

Option A — Clear app data

  1. Open Settings on your Android phone.
  2. Go to Apps → Mango.
  3. Tap Storage.
  4. Tap Clear data (or Clear storage).

Option B — Uninstall the app

  1. Long-press the Mango app icon.
  2. Select Uninstall.

Logging out from the Profile screen only signs you out. Your data remains on the device until you clear data or uninstall.

What is deleted

  • Your phone number and profile name
  • All farms, crops, and crop seasons
  • All activity, wage, and payment records
  • Worker and partner contact details you entered
  • All balances and profit-share settings

What is kept

  • Mango does not retain any copy of your data on a developer server.
  • If Android backup was enabled for your device, Google may have backed up app data separately under your Google account. You can manage backups in your device Google account settings.

Recovery

Deletion is permanent. Deleted on-device data cannot be recovered by Mango.
