Delete your Mango account and data

App: Mango (com.farmlog.mango)
Developer: Ashish Patel

Mango stores your farms, people, and money records in your private online workspace. Deleting your account removes that server-held data.

From the app

  1. Sign in with the Google account you use for Mango.
  2. Open You.
  3. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

If you cannot open the app

Email aspatel@tripactions.com from the Google account used to sign in and ask for account deletion. We will delete the matching Mango account and workspace records.

What is deleted

  • Your Mango Auth account
  • All farms, crops, and crop seasons
  • All activity, wage, and payment records
  • Worker and partner contact details you entered
  • All balances and profit-share settings

What is kept

  • Your Google account itself is not deleted.
  • If Android backup was enabled, Google may have backed up app data separately. Manage backups in your device Google account settings.

After deletion

Deletion is permanent. Signing in again creates a new empty workspace.

Questions

Email aspatel@tripactions.com.
