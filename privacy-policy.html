Mango Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026
App: Mango (com.farmlog.mango)
Developer: Ashish Patel

This policy describes how the Mango Android app handles information.

Summary

Mango is an offline farm-management app. Your data stays on your Android device. Mango does not operate a backend server and does not sell your data.

Information the app stores on your device

  • Phone number — used to sign in to your on-device workspace
  • Name — your profile name and names of workers or partners you add
  • Phone numbers you enter — optional contact numbers for workers or partners
  • Financial and farm records — farms, crops, activities, wages, payments, and balances
  • Voice input — if you tap the microphone button, the app uses the microphone to help enter an amount. Audio is processed for that entry and is not sent to Mango servers.

Sign-in

The current beta uses a test OTP flow. You enter a 10-digit phone number and a 4-digit code. No real SMS is sent and no verification server is used.

How data is stored

Data is saved locally in an app data file on your phone. Mango does not transmit your farm or financial records to developer servers. The app does not request internet access for this purpose.

Android backup

Mango allows Android backup. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in backups managed by Google. See your device and Google account settings for backup controls.

Sharing and selling

Mango does not share or sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

Permissions

  • Microphone — optional, only when you choose voice entry

Children

Mango is intended for adults managing farm operations and is not directed at children under 18.

Deleting your data

See Delete account and data for instructions to remove all Mango data from your device.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when that happens.
